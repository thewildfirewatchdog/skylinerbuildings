## Storyboard notes
- Two cameras where possible: one on Jeremy and the fob, one on the machine, so the cause-and-effect of each button is undeniable. Show the fob and the machine in the same frame at least once (wide shot at 0:14).
- No music over the engine start; the sound is the proof.
- On-screen text stays under eight words per card.
- Do not show customer properties without a release; the Metolius install may be referenced by name only if the customer agrees, otherwise say "a three-building system in Central Oregon".

## Shot list (new footage required)
1. Wide: pump + tank + hose + Jeremy at 100 ft, fob visible (16:9, tripod).
2. Fob close-up: A, B, C, D presses, one at a time, with pauses (macro, tripod).
3. Engine crank and catch, close (real audio, second camera).
4. Throttle lever moving idle → full on C (close).
5. Valve actuator turning and water discharge starting on D (close, then wide).
6. Water arc over a roofline or onto defensible space (wide, backlit if possible).
7. Stop on B: engine dies, throttle drops, valve closes; stopwatch overlay for failsafe timing (two cameras).
8. Signal-loss failsafe: pull the Remote Port plug while running; same three things happen.
9. Manual overrides: start and stop from the pump switches.
10. Beauty pans of the skid: Honda badge, pump end, control box label, Deutsch port, battery, solar panel.
11. Flow meter and pressure gauge readings during the wet test (close, legible).
12. Range test: fob press at measured 50 / 100 / 150 ft with a tape or rangefinder in frame.
13. Shop b-roll: harness building, relay box, receivers, labeling, crimping.
14. Crating: pallet, corner posts, strapping, liftgate.
15. Jeremy to camera, two takes, clean background.
16. What's-in-the-box flat-lay: fobs, manual, certificate, labels.
Not to be filmed or staged: fire, embers, smoke, or anyone "protecting a home during a fire."
