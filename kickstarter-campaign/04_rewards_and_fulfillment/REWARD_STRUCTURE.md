# Reward Structure — Dry Lightning 3000 Kickstarter (DRAFT v1, 2026-09-17)

Status: PROPOSED. Prices, caps and delivery windows are draft decisions for Jeremy and the advisor. All numbers tie to `03_financial_model/WW_Kickstarter_Financial_Model_v1.xlsx` (Inputs section C, Rewards sheet).

Shipping region for every physical reward: **contiguous United States only** (no Alaska, Hawaii, Canada or international). Reasons: freight cost on a 165 lb crate, engine emissions/registration differences, and the product-liability policy being sought is a US policy. Kickstarter's pledge maximum for US projects ($10,000) rules out a two-pump tier.

Delivery windows assume a 2027-01-19 launch, 30-day campaign (ends 2027-02-18), payout about 2027-03-04, parts ordered the same week, 3-week engine/pump-end lead time, and founder-built assembly at roughly 4 pumps per week (Capacity sheet). Windows include a one-month buffer.

### Tier 1 — Supporter — Backer Wall + Decal ($35)
Includes: name on the "Founding Backers" page at wildfirewatchdog.com (opt-in), one weatherproof Wildfire Watchdog decal, all backer updates including the build log and test videos.
Excludes: any equipment.
Ongoing costs: none.

### Tier 2 — Remote Control Kit ($1,250 + $25 shipping, limit 10)
Includes: the same four-channel rolling-code remote system used on the DL3000, packaged as a retrofit for a pump the backer already owns: two Solidremote 202U rolling-code receivers, one TX-134 four-button fob (plus one spare fob), sealed relay enclosure with three 12V relays and manual override switches, pre-terminated harness with Deutsch DT 6-pin Remote Port, inline fuses, 2 in. 12V spring-return electric ball valve (normally closed), printed installation guide, phone/email installation support.
Compatibility (must be stated plainly on the reward): **Honda iGX390 electric-start engines only** in v1 (the engine the prototype uses). NorthStar 420cc compatibility is planned but NOT yet bench-tested (briefing 2026-09-10 lists it as an open action), so it is not promised. Backer must have a 12V battery on the pump and be comfortable with basic 12V wiring or hire a small-engine shop.
Excludes: engine, pump, battery, hoses, installation.
Ongoing costs: fob battery (CR2032-type, ~$2/yr).
Why it exists: lets people who already own a good pump back the project at a lower price, and it is the accessory the business plan wants to sell as a standalone SKU anyway. Capped at 10 to limit support load.

### Tier 3 — Dry Lightning 3000 — Early Bird ($4,699 + $349 freight, limit 10)
Includes (the "DL3000 v1 base package", identical in tiers 3, 4, 5):

- 12V battery, USB power bank and solar maintainer panel

- Phone and email support from the builder
Excludes: fuel, suction hose and strainer, discharge hose and nozzle, sprinklers, water tank, installation, any app/internet/monitoring feature.
Ongoing costs: fuel and engine maintenance (oil, filter, spark plug) per Honda schedule; fob battery. No subscriptions.
Delivery: June 2027, contiguous US, LTL freight to a residential address with liftgate. Backer must be able to receive a 165 lb crate.

## What is deliberately NOT offered
- Sprinkler systems, roof sprinklers, manifolds or "complete house protection" kits. The insurer question "do you manufacture or sell sprinkler systems?" was answered No for this SKU; adding them changes the insurance class.
- Phone / app / cloud control, sensors, auto-start on smoke or heat, or monitoring. These exist as a demonstrated prototype but are not FCC-declared or insured for sale. They are described in the campaign as the next development stage, not as a reward.
- Trailer or pump-house systems (freight, install-heavy, semi-custom).
- Two-pump bundles (above the $10,000 pledge cap).
- Stretch goals that add product scope. If the campaign overfunds, the only "stretch" is opening more units of tiers 4 and 5 up to the 35-pump cap.
- Any tier that implies installation or site service.

## Reward-page wording that must appear on every pump tier
"Preparedness equipment, not a guarantee. This product does not make property fireproof, does not guarantee protection, does not replace defensible space, firefighters or evacuation orders, and is not a monitoring, alarm or fire-detection device. The remote operates the pump only while an operator is present to supervise it. Requires a water source, fuel, hoses and a 12V battery (battery included)."

## Sales-tax and shipping notes
- Kickstarter does not collect sales tax. Oregon has no sales tax. Backers in other states may owe use tax; state that in the FAQ.
- Shipping is entered per reward as a flat US amount, so it is visible to backers and inside the fee base.
- Freight to a business address with a dock is cheaper; offer a $60 refund by backer survey for dock delivery (PROPOSED).
