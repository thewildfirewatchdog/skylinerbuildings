## Limited Warranty — Dry Lightning 3000 (v1)
1. **Coverage.** Wildfire Watchdog LLC ("we") warrants to the original backer that the DL3000 frame, harness, relay enclosure, remote receivers and fobs, discharge valve assembly and pump end are free from defects in materials and workmanship for **12 months** from the delivery date.
2. **Engine.** The Honda iGX390 engine is covered by Honda's own limited warranty through authorized Honda dealers. We will help you find a dealer and provide the build record.
3. **What we will do.** For a covered defect we will, at our option, ship a replacement part for backer-replaceable items (fob, receiver, relay, fuse, valve actuator, switches, battery within 90 days) or repair/replace the unit at our shop in Bend, Oregon. Freight to and from our shop for warranty service is the backer's responsibility unless we agree otherwise in writing.
4. **Not covered.** Normal wear; fuel, oil, filters, plugs; damage from running dry, freezing, dead-heading without the relief path, contaminated water, incorrect fuel, improper storage, modification, or use outside the Quick Start Guide; cosmetic damage; consequential losses of any kind, including fire damage to property.
5. **Claim process.** Email photos/video and the serial number to admin@wildfirewatchdog.com; we respond within 2 business days.
6. **Optional extended warranty** (add-on, $350): extends section 1 to 24 months; same terms.
7. **Limitation.** To the extent permitted by law, this warranty is the exclusive remedy. Some states do not allow limits on implied warranties or incidental/consequential damages; those limits may not apply to you.

## Remote Control Kit warranty
Same terms, 12 months, parts only (kit is installed by the backer); we provide phone/email installation support but do not warrant the backer's pump or engine.

## Backer terms specific to this campaign
- Kickstarter is not a store. Rewards are estimated, not guaranteed, delivery dates; we will post an update at least every two weeks during production and immediately if a date moves.
- Shipping: contiguous US only. A backer who cannot receive a 165 lb liftgate freight delivery may nominate a nearby business address.
- Refunds: before parts are ordered (until about March 10, 2027), we will refund in full on request through Kickstarter's tools. After parts are ordered, refunds are at our discretion less non-recoverable costs. If we cannot deliver at all, we will refund what remains after documented costs, as Kickstarter's terms require creators to do.
- Safety acknowledgement (checkbox in the backer survey): "I understand this is preparedness equipment, not a guarantee of protection; it requires an operator present, a water source, fuel and hoses; it is not a substitute for evacuation, defensible space or fire services."
- Sales/use tax: not collected; backers outside Oregon may owe use tax in their state.
- Data: we use backer name/address only to fulfill the reward and for warranty; no resale of data.
