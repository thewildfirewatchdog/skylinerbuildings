## 4. Patent status

**Filings.** Business plan 2026-09-15: USPTO provisional 63/958,172 "Remote-start gas water pump with automatic choke" and 63/958,210 "Automatic wildfire sprinkler program," both filed 2026-01-11. Non-provisional must be filed by 2027-01-11 (plan target 2027-01-08). Open USPTO action: micro-entity certification plus surcharge on 63/958,210 due 2026-09-23. Cost budgeted for self-filed non-provisional, micro entity: $1,500.

**Drive documents.** FINAL_Complete_USPTO_Patent_40_Claims (1).pdf (document date 2025-12-20, inventor Jeremy John Coon; "Watchdog AutoPump System"; 40 claims, 5 independent: 1, 8, 9, 10, 35). Second draft, "final provisional patent for wildfire watchdog.pdf" (2025-12-16), covers the multi-zone roof/eave/ground sprinkler system with solar power, environmental sensors and flow verification; inventor field still "[Your Name]" in that draft. Folders 11_Patent_and_Invention_Notes and 10_Patent_And_Invention are empty; 08_Patent_and_Legal holds the two PDFs, a "provisional patent filings" figure folder and the Dry_Thunder_3000 firmware folder.

**What the 40-claim application covers (5 bullets):**
- Remote start system for a carbureted engine built on a motorized choke actuator (servo, 0/90/180 degrees), two relays (ignition ground, starter), a microcontroller running a timed choke/crank sequence, a wireless interface and a 12V supply (claims 1, 10-17, 28-30).
- Multi-protocol, MAC-authenticated wireless control: ESP-NOW mesh, local WiFi AP webpage, cloud platforms (Tago.io, AWS IoT, etc.), multi-receiver broadcast, self-healing mesh, physical override buttons (claims 2-7, 18-20, 32, 39).
- Freeze protection: temperature-triggered drain valve with redundant dual-controller logic and ESP-NOW alerts (claims 8, 21-23, 31).
- Multi-pump tank transfer: float-switch-triggered secondary pump via wireless link (claims 9, 24-25).
- Monitoring and logging: vibration, flow meter and camera verification with local storage, local webpage and protocol-agnostic cloud logging; engine-state-adaptive choke; ML-optimized start schedule (claims 33-38, 40).

**Not covered.** Per RF briefing 2026-09-10 sec. 5: the RF key-fob v1 control "is unaffected by and doesn't apply to" the provisionals; "not novel enough to patent on its own." Also note: the claims center on a servo choke actuator and dual relays, while Current_Parts_List.md (2026-06-24) states the iGX390 has "built-in auto choke — no choke servo needed" and the Dry Thunder firmware uses throttle/run/starter/valve relays. Whether the shipped DL3000 practices claim 1 as drafted is an open question for patent counsel.
