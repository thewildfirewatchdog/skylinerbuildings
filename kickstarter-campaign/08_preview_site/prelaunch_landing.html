A wildfire water pump you start from your porch.

Dry Lightning 3000 — Honda-powered, key-fob start, throttle and valve. Coming to Kickstarter January 19, 2027. Ten Early Bird units.

  • Start, throttle, open the valve and stop from about 150 ft. No app, no internet.
  • Failsafe wiring: lose signal or power and it shuts itself down.
  • Built and wet-tested in Bend, Oregon. Lower-48 shipping.
